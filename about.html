About Us

What is AIAA?

The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA) is the world's largest aerospace professional society, bringing together students, researchers, engineers, industry leaders, and innovators across the aerospace sector.


AIAA supports the advancement of aerospace science, engineering, and technology through professional development, networking, publications, conferences, competitions, and educational initiatives.

Our Branch

The McMaster AIAA Student Branch was established to create a hub for aerospace-related activities on campus. We aim to connect students from all disciplines who share an interest in aviation, space exploration, aerospace engineering, and emerging technologies.

Our Goals

  • Unite and grow McMaster's aerospace community
  • Provide professional development opportunities for students
  • Connect members with industry professionals and researchers
  • Support participation in aerospace competitions and projects
  • Promote aerospace research and innovation
