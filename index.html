Welcome to McMaster AIAA

The McMaster AIAA Student Branch is a student-led community dedicated to advancing aerospace education, innovation, and professional development.

Our Mission

We aim to build McMaster’s aerospace community through hands-on projects, technical competitions, industry engagement, and collaborative learning opportunities.

As an official student branch of the world’s largest aerospace technical society, our mission aligns with AIAA’s vision of being the voice of the aerospace profession through innovation, technical excellence, and global leadership.

    What is AIAA?

    The American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics is the world’s largest aerospace professional society, bringing together students, researchers, engineers, industry leaders, and innovators across the aerospace sector.

    Our branch connects McMaster students from every discipline who share an interest in aviation, space exploration, aerospace engineering, and emerging technologies.
