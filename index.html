Welcome to the McMaster AIAA Student Branch

The McMaster AIAA Student Branch is a student-led community dedicated to advancing aerospace education, innovation, and professional development. As an official student branch of the world's largest aerospace technical society, our mission aligns with AIAA's vision of being the voice of the aerospace profession through innovation, technical excellence, and global leadership.

Our Mission

We aim to build McMaster's aerospace community through hands-on projects, technical competitions, industry engagement, and collaborative learning opportunities.
