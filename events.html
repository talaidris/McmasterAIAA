Events, Initiatives,& Projects

  • September 24, 2026

    Kickoff Event

    We hosted our very first kickoff event for the McMaster AIAA Student Branch, welcoming students to hear firsthand perspectives on space exploration and the aerospace industry, while connecting with professionals in the field.

    • Alice Bowman, Mission Operations Manager for NASA’s New Horizons mission, took us through the journey of the New Horizons space probe, what lies ahead for the mission, and the challenges of operating a spacecraft billions of kilometres from Earth.
    • Stephen Carnduff, Technical Expert in Stability & Control at Bombardier, walked us through the supersonic flight-testing program for the Global 8000 and explored why the aircraft had to be pushed beyond Mach 1.
    • We also thank Walter Gordon, Chairman of the AIAA Niagara Frontier Section and former development manager and engineer with Moog Space and Defense Group, for joining us and helping make this event possible.

    Thank you to our speakers, our executive team, and everyone who came out and helped make our first major event a success. Stay tuned for what’s ahead for McMaster AIAA!
